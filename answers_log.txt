--- AGENT 1: LOCAL JOTHIDAM ANSWER ---

மேலும், நட்சத்திரங்களின் பரந்த விளக்கம் மற்றும் அவற்றின் பொருள் ஜோதிடத்தின் அடித்தளமாகக் கருதப்படுகிறது. 

மொத்தத்தில், நட்சத்திரம் என்பது ஒருவர் தனிப்பட்ட ஜோதிடத்திற்கான அடிப்படை கூறாகும்.

--- AGENT 2: WEB SEARCH ANSWER ---
A Nakshatra is one of the 27 sectors of the sky in Vedic astrology, representing a lunar mansion. Each Nakshatra correlates with a particular star or group of stars and plays a significant role in traditional Hindu astrology. The term "Nakshatra" is derived from Sanskrit words "Naksha" (map) and "Tara" (star). 

In Vedic astrology, the Nakshatras are fundamental for determining various aspects of a person's horoscope, including timing for important events and characteristics related to individuals based on their birth Nakshatra. The lunar month is divided into these Nakshatras, where each one is associated with specific qualities and traits.

For further details, you can refer to sources like [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakshatra), [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/nakshatra), and [Rudraksha Ratna](https://www.rudraksha-ratna.com/articles/27-nakshatras-constellations?srsltid=AYCX9vKiDNqUdSfRJTbL1Jj6hrZWtcGrxGSy2DTpzVrLHceDMPVdM284).
